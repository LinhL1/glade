# Glade

A simple daily gratitude log + practice. Every day, write down three things you're grateful for...each entry adds a clover to your garden.

**[Open Glade →](https://linhl1.github.io/glade/)**

## Features

- **Daily journaling** — three small things, every day
- **A garden** — each entry adds a random clover to ur garden
- **Calendar view** — browse your whole history of entries by month
- **Works offline** — installs like a real app and works with no internet connection
- **Daily reminders** — an optional notification each evening at 9PM to remind you in case you missed it (based on your local time)
- **Share your entries** — send a day's gratitude to someone else
- **No account needed** — everything stays on your device, nothing to sign up for

## How to get it on your phone

### iPhone (Safari)
1. Open the link above in **Safari** (must be Safari, not another browser).
2. Tap the **Share** icon at the bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

### Android (Chrome)
1. Open the link above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app** if it appears, or open the **⋮** menu and tap **Add to Home Screen** / **Install app**.
3. Confirm.

Once installed, Glade opens from your home screen like any other app that also works offline :)
